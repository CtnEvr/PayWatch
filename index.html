PayWatch

Subscription tracking made simple.

English

PayWatch helps you track subscriptions, renewal dates, and recurring costs in one simple place.

Designed with privacy in mind, PayWatch does not require an account and does not collect personal data. Your data stays on your device and in your iCloud account for sync across iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and widgets.

Español

Diseñada pensando en la privacidad, PayWatch no requiere una cuenta y no recopila datos personales. Tus datos permanecen en tu dispositivo y en tu cuenta de iCloud para sincronización entre iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch y widgets.
