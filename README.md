# PayWatch

PayWatch is an iOS app that helps you track subscriptions, renewal dates, and recurring costs across your Apple devices.

## Available Pages

- `index.html` — app overview
- `support.html` — support information
- `privacy.html` — privacy policy

## Platforms

PayWatch is available on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and widgets.

## Privacy

PayWatch does not require an account and does not use third-party analytics.  
Your data stays on your device and in your iCloud account for sync across Apple devices.
